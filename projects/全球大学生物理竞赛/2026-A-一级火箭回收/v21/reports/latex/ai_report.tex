\subsection*{1. System and scope of assistance}
OpenAI ChatGPT with Codex tools was used in preparing this paper. The exact deployed model identifier is not available in the retained record. Assistance included discussing modeling alternatives and limitations, checking equations and units, searching for references, translating and editing text, preparing LaTeX, arranging tables and figures, inserting the user-provided photograph, and writing the explanatory code in Appendix A. It is therefore not restricted to spelling correction. The five reference listings were generated during the current revision and passed the elementary tests described in the electronic package. Those tests do not independently reproduce the flight tables or establish mission-level feasibility.

The photograph was extracted from the uploaded RAR archive and inserted without image generation or pixel editing. ChatGPT was not used to synthesize new flight curves in this revision. The team remains responsible for understanding, verifying, explaining, and defending all equations, code, numerical results, references, and conclusions.
