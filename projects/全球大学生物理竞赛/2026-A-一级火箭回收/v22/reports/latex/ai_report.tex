\section{AI Usage Report}
\label{sec:ai}
\subsection{System Used}
OpenAI ChatGPT with Codex tools was used as an assistant during the preparation of this paper. The exact deployed model version is not recorded in the available conversation archive.

\subsection{Scope and Nature of Use}
The system assisted with discussion of physical assumptions and numerical methods, equation and unit checks, identification of references, translation and academic editing, and LaTeX preparation. It also helped arrange figures and tables, insert the uploaded recovery photograph, and prepare the explanatory Python listings in Appendix~\ref{app:code}. The photograph was extracted from the supplied image archive and was not generated or retouched by the AI system.

\subsection{Content Authorship}
The trajectory tables and numerical conclusions retain the modeling results provided for this study. AI assistance also contributed to the method explanations and reference-code implementation; it was not confined to grammar correction. The new listings are explanatory components rather than the programs that generated the flight tables. Their tests therefore do not establish independent reproduction of the mission results. Responsibility for the scientific content, interpretation, and final submission remains with Team~\TeamNumber.

\subsection{Quality Assurance}
The final LaTeX document was compiled and checked for unresolved references, missing characters, layout overflow, consistent team numbering, and the five-page code appendix. The reference code was checked against smooth-ODE RK4 convergence, thrust--mass-flow consistency, drag direction, fuel-exhaustion handling, touchdown interpolation, and a bounded search test. These checks have a narrower scope than full trajectory validation. The competition also requires exact AI inputs and complete outputs \cite{airules}; retained exchanges accompany the working archive, but the complete historical transcript is not yet available. It must be added to the submission record before full compliance with that disclosure requirement can be asserted.
